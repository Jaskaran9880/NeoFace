Place your face photos in this folder.

## Requirements
- Front-facing photos (look at camera)
- Good lighting (no shadows on face)
- Neutral expression
- JPG, PNG, or HEIC format
- At least 5-10 photos for good enrollment

## Tips
- Use photos from different angles (slight left/right)
- Avoid sunglasses or hats
- Avoid blurry photos
- Phone selfies work great

## After adding photos
Run the enrollment tool:
```powershell
python tools\enroll_fast.py
```
